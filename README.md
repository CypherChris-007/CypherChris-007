## 🧭 About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at **The University of Texas at San Antonio**, building on an A.S. in CS (**Cum Laude**) from Northwest Vista College. My current focus sits at the intersection of **AI application development** and **enterprise systems administration** — I'm building local, privacy-respecting LLM agents with LangChain and Ollama while also standing up simulated Active Directory domains that provision users end to end.

I bring two internships of hands-on IT experience — high-volume helpdesk resolution and a redesigned troubleshooting methodology that eliminated lab downtime — into everything I build, with a bias toward clean, maintainable, well-documented systems.
